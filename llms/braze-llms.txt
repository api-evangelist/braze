## Frequently Asked Questions

[What are BrazeAI™ Agents?](https://www.braze.com/product/ai-agents): BrazeAI™ Agents are autonomous AI assistants built into the Braze platform that help marketers work faster and smarter. They are highly configurable and can be deployed across the Braze platform, using your data and real-time context to automate tasks, personalize campaigns at scale, and help improve results across channels like email, push, in-app, and more.

[What types of agents does Braze offer?](https://www.braze.com/product/ai-agents): Braze offers three complementary types of agents. Pre-built agents are ready to use for common marketing tasks like content optimization and campaign QA. Custom agents are created in the BrazeAI Agent Console™, and let you design task-specific, LLM-powered assistants that plug in directly in your Braze workflows. AI decisioning agents, available through BrazeAI Decisioning Studio™, make decisions at the individual customer level to optimize any business KPI. These agents can personalize channel, offer, message, creative, timing, frequency and more. They autonomously experiment and continuously learn from every customer interaction.

[How does the BrazeAI Agent Console™ work?](https://www.braze.com/product/ai-agents): The Agent Console is an intuitive dashboard where marketers can create and manage custom agents without coding. You define the task, choose an AI model, and provide instructions (in the form of a prompt) for the agent. Agents are then invoked in either catalogs or canvases, to enrich data or personalize experiences, respectively. Currently, we are seeing customers use BrazeAI™ Agents to score leads, translate and localize content, and adapt communications based on customer behavior, and we are excited to see how else you put BrazeAI™ Agents to work for you!

[How are custom agents different from conventional marketing automation?](https://www.braze.com/product/ai-agents): While traditional marketing automation runs on fixed rules and workflows defined in advance, custom agents bring AI models directly into Braze to generate outputs and route customers dynamically through canvases. Instead of just following if/then logic, agents can enrich data, personalize content, and adapt to customer behavior in real time, giving marketers more flexibility and intelligence than conventional automation alone.

[What are common use cases for BrazeAI™ Agents?](https://www.braze.com/product/ai-agents): Brands can use BrazeAI™ Agents to tackle a range of marketing challenges, from driving conversions with personalized recommendations to reducing churn through timely offers, while also improving efficiency with tasks like scaling localization, enriching customer profiles, and automating campaign QA checks. Because agents can be shaped to fit each brand’s needs, they make it possible to scale everyday tasks and complex personalization in ways never before possible with Braze.

[Which AI models power BrazeAI™ Agents?](https://www.braze.com/product/ai-agents): BrazeAI™ Agents support leading large language models from providers like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google Gemini, giving marketers flexibility to choose their preferred LLM. AI decisioning agents rely on reinforcement learning, a type of AI best-fit for marketing personalization that optimizes customer and business value. With the Braze Model Context Protocol (MCP), you can also securely connect external AI tools to your Braze data for more advanced use cases.

[Who is Braze built for?](https://www.braze.com/pricing): Braze supports companies at every stage of growth including startups launching their first engagement programs, to scaling companies meeting expansive growth, to large global enterprises looking for the leading customer engagement platform.

[What will my contract with Braze look like?](https://www.braze.com/pricing): Your Braze contract will be comprised of a Platform Edition tailored to your business needs, Monthly Active Users and Profiles, an allotment of Action Credits to power messaging and AI features, and CX Services to support your use of the platform. We also offer a variety of add-on SKUs across our Braze Data Platform and BrazeAITM product suite so you can customize your contract and unlock other valuable features within Braze.

[What is a Monthly Active User? What is a Profile? Why are they important?](https://www.braze.com/pricing): A Monthly Active User (MAU) is any unique person who records a session on your Braze-connected app or website during a calendar month. You decide who counts: you can track every visitor if you value anonymous traffic, or limit tracking to only logged-in users. A Profile is the digital record of that person. It stores the user traits and behaviors that allow you to send truly personalized messages. Because your total user base is typically larger than your active monthly audience, every MAU in your plan includes a large allotment of User Profiles. This allows you to store data and re-engage customers with the right message, even if they haven't visited your app or website in a while.

[What products and channels does Action Credits give me access to?](https://www.braze.com/pricing): Braze Action Credits unlock the flexible use of any of the following features throughout your contract: Email, SMS/MMS, RCS, WhatsApp, LINE, KakaoTalk, Content Cards, Banners, Message Archiving, Audience Sync, Webhooks, and BrazeAI Agent Console™. New and exciting features may be added to Action Credits in the future, giving you instant access to use them upon release.

[How do Action Credits work?](https://www.braze.com/pricing): Think of Action Credits as a single, flexible currency for your Braze account. Instead of purchasing fixed quantities for individual products, you buy one pool of credits as part of your contract. You can then use those credits across different channels and features based on your engagement strategy. Each action consumes a different number of credits—for example, sending an SMS may cost 1 credit, while a WhatsApp message may cost 5. Because all usage draws from the same pool, you can easily shift spend (e.g., from Email to Agent Console) without needing to contract a new product. Credits give you the flexibility to adapt your strategy as your customer needs change and your business strategy evolves.

[How will I be able to track my usage?](https://www.braze.com/pricing): Braze has comprehensive reporting and billing dashboards to help you monitor performance throughout your contract.

[What kind of support will I get?](https://www.braze.com/pricing): Braze offers a variety of Support and Service plans to fit customer needs including 24x7x365 Customer support for the world's leading brands. From initial implementation to long-term strategy, we deliver support and partnership, wherever you need it, when you need it. Check out our Support & Services to learn more: https://www.braze.com/customer-support-services

[Does Braze offer a free plan?](https://www.braze.com/pricing): Braze does not offer a free plan at this time. Companies can still explore the platform through trials, startup programs, and other tailored entry plans. Speak to your account manager or contact us for more details.

[What is the Braze Creative Studio?](https://www.braze.com/product/creative-studio): The Creative Studio is a built-in hub for creative tools designed to help you move from idea to execution faster. It serves as a single, centralized place for your media library, templates, content blocks, brand guidelines, calendar, and dynamic content feeds. The Creative Studio is designed to be flexible, so you can pull in creative assets and content from a wide variety of sources and only use the specific features you need.

[How do the Figma and Canva integrations work?](https://www.braze.com/product/creative-studio): These integrations bridge the gap between design and delivery by connecting your creative tools directly to Braze. With the Figma integration, you can send designs to your Braze Media Library in minutes. The Canva integration lets you sync finished visuals and full emails to your Media Library in seconds. Both integrations ensure your marketing team is always working with the latest approved assets—no manual downloads, re-uploads, or developer tickets required.

[How does the Creative Studio speed up creative execution?](https://www.braze.com/product/creative-studio): The Creative Studio eliminates the "asset wrangling" bottleneck by streamlining the path from design to deployment. Instead of manual handoffs, a designer can update a banner in Figma or Canva and sync it directly to the Media Library, ensuring the latest creative is ready for use without any reworks. Marketing managers can then make quick brand updates to cross-channel templates and content blocks simultaneously, accelerating production for the entire team. The Creative Studio also provides a single place to manage connected content, catalogs, and promotion codes, ensuring that enriching campaigns with dynamic content is seamless and accurate.
